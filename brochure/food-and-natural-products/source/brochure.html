About R.K. Trading

One trader, both directions.

R.K. Trading is a Mumbai-based general trader of food and natural products. Dates, dry fruits, chocolates, herbals and ayurvedic products come in; spices, tea, grains, fresh produce and FMCG go out. Since 2017 we have sourced, graded, packed and shipped, so buyers deal with one accountable partner from enquiry to delivery.
