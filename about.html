About R.K. Trading

R.K. Trading is a Mumbai-based general trader that connects Indian exporters and international buyers with imported industrial and natural products. Since 2017 we have sourced, graded, packed and shipped, so buyers deal with one accountable partner from enquiry to delivery.

Our story

A trading business built on clear dealings.

R.K. Trading was founded in Mumbai in September 2017 and is run as a sole proprietorship by Riyaz Ahmed Wani. Today we trade in both directions: heating equipment, building materials, herbals and dates come in, and spices, tea, grains, fresh produce and FMCG go out.

We are a general trader. We source products from manufacturers and growers and supply them to buyers. We are not an authorised distributor or agent of any brand, which is why we describe products by type and specification rather than by brand name.

Grading, sizing and packing are done by us, and we keep origin and shelf-life details for each product so we can confirm them in every quotation.
