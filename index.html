FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

How do I get a price?
Pricing is on quotation only. Send an enquiry with the product, quantity and destination and we will come back to you with a quote.
What are your MOQ, lead times and payment terms?
These depend on the product and destination. We share minimum order quantity, lead time and terms on request, by phone. Include your phone or WhatsApp number in your enquiry and we will call you.
Which countries do you import from and export to?
We import from China, Turkey, Afghanistan, Dubai (UAE), Oman and Italy, and export to the Middle East, Malaysia and Sri Lanka. If you need another origin or destination, ask us.
Do you supply branded products?
R.K. Trading is a general trader. We are not an authorised distributor or agent of any brand, so we describe products by type and specification, not by brand name. Tell us what you need and we will confirm what we can supply.
Who does the packing, grading and sizing?
We do. Grading, sizing and packing are handled by us before every shipment.
Can you confirm origin and shelf life?
Yes. We hold origin and shelf-life details for each product and confirm them in your quotation.
What happens after I send an enquiry?
Your enquiry reaches our team directly. We review your requirement and get back to you using the contact details you gave us.
