  • Branded waters

Product details
Product range

Snacks, Biscuits & Confectionery

  • Chocolates

Product details
Product range

Rice, Grains & Staples

Everyday grocery staples sourced to grade, packaging size and destination-market labelling requirements.

  • Basmati & non-basmati rice
  • Atta & flours
  • Dals & pulses
  • Cooking oil
  • Spices & masalas

Examples: Basmati rice, wheat atta, toor and moong dal, whole and ground spices.

Product details
Rice
Basmati (long grain, steam or sella) and non-basmati varieties; specified by grain length, broken percentage, moisture and purity
Atta & flours
Wheat atta, maida, suji and other flours, milled to the buyer’s specification
Dals & pulses
Toor, moong, chana, masoor, urad and others, whole or split, polished or unpolished
Oils & spices
Edible oils in retail packs or bulk; whole and ground spices and masalas, including turmeric, cumin, coriander and chilli
Packing
1 kg to 50 kg bags (PP, non-woven or paper), retail packs in master cartons, private-label packing on request
Shelf life & storage
Dry, clean, ventilated storage; fumigation where the destination market requires it
Key documents
Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, phytosanitary and fumigation certificates where applicable; spice shipments follow Spices Board requirements

Afghanistan and other source markets

Botanical Roots & Herbs

Unprocessed botanical ingredients and dried plant materials supplied as raw commodities for further processing.

  • Licorice (mulethi) root
  • Dried herbs
  • Selected botanicals

Specifications: part used, grade, moisture, cut size and packing.

Product details
Main product
Licorice (mulethi) root, with other dried herbs and botanicals available on request
Forms
Whole roots, cut pieces, slices and powder, depending on the intended processing
Specification points
Part used, cut size, moisture, foreign matter and any content or test parameters set by the buyer
Packing
Jute or PP bags, or pressed bales, with weights agreed per consignment
Availability
Depends on harvest season and origin; confirmed at quotation
Intended use
Supplied as a raw commodity for further processing; end-use rules vary by destination market
Key documents
Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, phytosanitary certificate, fumigation certificate and lab test reports where required

Vegetables & Agri Commodities

Fresh agricultural commodities for importers, wholesalers, distributors and food businesses.

Products
Red onions, tomatoes and other selected vegetables and agricultural commodities

Documentation & compliance

Paperwork handled properly.

The right documents, matched to the product and the destination market.

Requirements differ between processed FMCG, botanical raw materials and fresh produce, and between import markets. We review what each shipment needs up front so documentation does not become a delay at the port.

  • Commercial invoice
  • Packing list
  • Bill of lading / airway bill
  • Certificate of origin
  • Phytosanitary certificate (plants & produce)
  • Fumigation certificate (where required)
  • Food-safety & labelling compliance
  • Quality & inspection reports
  • Letter of credit / banking documents
  • Customs clearance support
